Program description

Tampa Bay HVAC operates an automated missed-call text-back system. When a customer calls the business and no one answers, the customer receives an SMS follow-up within about one minute so their request isn't lost. The customer then has a short automated text conversation collecting their name, service address, type of service needed, and urgency, and the business is notified of urgent requests. Messages are conversational and transactional only. We do not send marketing blasts.
